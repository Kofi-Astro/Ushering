Our Story

Built on Professionalism & Genuine Hospitality

GPS Ushering and Events was founded to bring order, elegance and warmth to events across Ghana. What began as a small team of dedicated ushers has grown into a trusted service for event planners, corporate organizations, couples, funeral organizers, schools, churches and brands.

We believe that the first face a guest sees sets the tone for the entire event — so we train every usher to combine sharp presentation with genuine hospitality, ensuring each guest feels welcomed, guided and valued from arrival to departure.
