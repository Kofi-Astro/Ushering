1. What's Been Built So Far

Your website is built and working. It has seven pages, all styled in your brand colours (black, gold, burgundy/wine and white), fully mobile-friendly, and with a light/dark mode switch guests can toggle in the top menu.

  • Home — introduction, service highlights, gallery preview, testimonials, and a call to book.
  • About Us — your story, mission/vision, and why clients choose you.
  • Services — all nine service types (weddings, corporate events, funerals, conferences, birthdays, concerts, churches/schools, brand activations, custom events) with details on what each includes.
  • Gallery — a filterable photo grid by event type, with a click-to-enlarge view.
  • Testimonials — client quotes and ratings.
  • FAQ — answers to the questions clients ask most.
  • Book Us — a booking/inquiry form, your contact details, a WhatsApp chat button, and a map of your location.
Every page currently uses placeholder details (a sample phone number, email, address, and photo icons instead of real pictures) so the design could move forward without waiting. Section 3 lists exactly what needs to replace these before the site goes live.

Two admin tools, for two different jobs

Beyond the public pages, two private tools were built so you can manage the site yourself once it's live, without needing a developer for routine updates:

  • Content Admin (at /admin on the website) — a simple form-based editor for your services, gallery photos, testimonials, FAQ answers, and contact details. Every change you save updates the live site automatically within a minute or two.
  • Booking Admin (a separate private dashboard) — every booking request submitted through the Book Us form lands here. You can see each enquiry's details and mark it as New, Contacted, Confirmed, Completed, or Cancelled, so nothing gets lost in email or WhatsApp chats.

3. Hosting & Domain Plan

The site needs a place to live on the internet (hosting) and an address for people to type in (a domain name). Here is the recommended setup and why:

Hosting

  • Cloudflare Pages — hosts the public website itself. It's fast, free for a site this size, and works well alongside Cloudflare's other services.
  • Railway — hosts the small booking-management system behind the scenes (where booking requests are stored and reviewed in the Booking Admin dashboard).

This combination was chosen instead of a single all-in-one host because it keeps costs low (both have generous free tiers for a site this size) while giving a real, dedicated place to manage booking enquiries — rather than them only existing as emails or WhatsApp messages that can get buried.

4. Managing the Site Going Forward

Day-to-day content updates

Once live, updating the site does not require touching any code:

  • Add or edit a service, gallery photo, testimonial, or FAQ answer through the Content Admin form editor.
  • Update your phone number, email, address, or social media links in one place, and they update everywhere on the site automatically.

Handling booking enquiries

Every submission through the Book Us form appears in the Booking Admin dashboard, protected by a private login. From there, each enquiry can be marked New, Contacted, Confirmed, Completed, or Cancelled — giving a clear running list of who has reached out and where things stand with each one.

Ongoing support

Occasional updates, troubleshooting, or new features can continue to be handled on request — there's no obligation for anything beyond what's needed at the time.
