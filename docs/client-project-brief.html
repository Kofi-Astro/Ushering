1. Understanding Your Project

GPS Ushering and Events provides professional ushering and event support — weddings, corporate events, funerals, conferences, birthdays, concerts and other special occasions — across Ghana, serving event planners, corporate organizations, couples, funeral organizers, schools, churches and brands.

The goal of this website is to present that work with the professionalism, elegance and reliability it deserves: a place for prospective clients to see past events, understand the range of services on offer, and reach out to book with confidence.

What the site will include

PagePurpose
HomeFirst impression, service highlights, and a clear path to booking
About UsYour story, mission and what sets your team apart
ServicesFull breakdown of every event type you support
GalleryPhotos from past events, filterable by category
TestimonialsClient reviews and social proof
FAQAnswers to the questions clients ask most before booking
Book Us / ContactBooking form, WhatsApp chat, phone/email, and a map to your location

Features included

  • Mobile-friendly design that looks sharp on any phone, tablet or laptop
  • Booking/inquiry form, with submissions organized in a private dashboard so nothing gets lost
  • One-tap WhatsApp chat button on every page
  • Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Google Maps location on the Book Us page
  • Search engine optimization (SEO) targeting terms like "ushering services in Ghana," "professional ushers in Accra" and "wedding ushers in Accra"
  • A private content editor so services, gallery photos, testimonials and FAQ answers can be updated without touching any code
